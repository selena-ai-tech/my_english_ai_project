T-money vs Climate Card in Seoul: How Many Trips Make the Pass Cheaper?

For a 1-day Seoul Climate Card, the simple break-even is 4 separately charged base-fare journeys if you already own the card, or 6 if you need to buy the ₩3,000 physical card. Calculations now use Seoul Metropolitan Government’s current ₩1,550 adult card base fare, with 2-, 3-, 5-, and 7-day thresholds plus transfer, coverage, and airport exceptions.
